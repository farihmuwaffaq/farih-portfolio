## Snapshot

This professional work connected recurring data preparation, reporting, dashboards, and performance nudges into a more repeatable analytics workflow. Outcomes are described qualitatively because approved internal measurements are not available for publication.

## The situation

Analytics across multiple clients drew from several digital platforms. Operational and client-facing teams needed consistent, timely performance information—not just isolated reports.

## The problem

Recurring preparation and reporting created repetitive work and made it harder to focus analyst time on interpretation and recommendations.

## My responsibility

I worked across KPI definition, data exploration, analysis, dashboarding, recurring reporting, strategic presentation, automation, and pipeline maintenance.

## Approach

1. Consolidated data from multiple sources.
2. Standardized processing and cleaning steps.
3. Built dashboards and recurring performance outputs.
4. Automated monthly reporting workflows.
5. Delivered daily performance nudges through an internal chatbot.
6. Converted findings into presentation-ready insights and recommendations.

## Solution

The workflow moved data from sources through a maintained processing layer into dashboards, reports, and daily decision signals. The public diagram communicates that system without exposing client data or implementation details.

## Outcome

The system reduced repetitive reporting work, improved consistency, and let analysts spend more time interpreting performance. No unverified time-saving figure is published.

## What I learned

Automation is useful only when KPI definitions, freshness checks, and escalation paths are equally clear. A future public iteration could include a fully synthetic walkthrough.
