## Snapshot

This professional case study covers operational and commercial analysis supporting production, distribution, and profit-oriented decisions. It intentionally excludes strong numeric claims that have not been cleared and fully defined.

## The situation

An FMCG operation was scaling production and expanding distribution beyond Modern Trade into General Trade.

## The problem

Production, channel, and commercial decisions needed a shared analytical view to support coordinated action.

## My responsibility

I analyzed operational and commercial data and produced decision-facing outputs using Python, Excel, and Looker Studio.

## Approach

I organized relevant data, reviewed production and channel patterns, connected operational signals to commercial views, and communicated findings for planning discussions.

## Solution

The output was a practical analysis and reporting workflow linking production, channel expansion, and supply-chain decisions.

## Outcome

The work supported production scaling, channel expansion, and operational coordination. No production-capacity or profit number is presented because baseline, comparison period, attribution, and profit definition require confirmation.

## What I learned

Operational metrics need explicit definitions and owners. Future publication should add only approved metrics with periods, methodology, and team-versus-individual attribution.
