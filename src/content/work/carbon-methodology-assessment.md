## Snapshot

This hiring assessment compared forest-carbon methodologies for a scenario involving an Indonesian logging concession and translated the selection into an implementation pathway.

## The situation

The scenario required connecting methodology choice with project boundaries, baseline development, monitoring, stakeholder engagement, verification, and certification.

## The problem

A methodology recommendation had to be evaluated against project conditions and converted into a practical plan.

## My responsibility

I reviewed options, modeled the supplied scenario, recommended approaches for that assessment context, and outlined implementation and monitoring requirements.

## Approach

The assessment compared methods and recommended VM0010 and MSAH-002 for the scenario presented, then mapped boundaries, baseline, monitoring, engagement, verification, and certification.

## Solution

The output was a methodology assessment and phased implementation plan rather than an operating carbon project.

## Outcome

The assessment completed the requested comparison and plan. Any emission-reduction numbers in the underlying exercise were hypothetical simulations, not forecasts or project results.

## What I learned

Methodology eligibility and regulation change. The recommendation must be rechecked against current Verra and Indonesian requirements before practical or public use.
